    Exploration is not a discovery claim.

    These views resample calibrated IMAGE data onto a common sky grid, not a precision motion measurement. SPHEREx's native pixels are about 6.15 arcsec; display resampling adds no resolution. Wavelength, PSF, background, flags, and astrometric residuals can mimic changes. Zodi is not subtracted and flag masks are not applied. Missing coverage or a missing Gaia counterpart is not evidence of Planet X.
